📜 Captain's Log

🏛️ Project Context

This game follows the *Odyssey* after the Trojan War. The route is simplified for gameplay. The challenges cover Greek hospitality (*xenia*), pride, loyalty, the gods, and Odysseus's ten-year return.

Made as an original educational adaptation of Homer's ancient epic. The characters and events come from the public-domain work.

Use “Rules” to show the teacher the rulebook. Play together by taking turns on one screen. Works offline after saving this HTML file.
